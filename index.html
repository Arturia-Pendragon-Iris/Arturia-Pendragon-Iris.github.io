I am Yuetan (Iris) Chu, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST), under the supervision of Prof. Xin Gao. My research focuses on medical image analysis, medical foundation models, and AI-enabled quantitative analysis of CT and X-ray tomography data, with applications in pulmonary, cardiovascular, and critical-care imaging.

My work covers degradation modeling and restoration for X-ray tomography, representation learning for medical visual foundation models, structured medical report normalization for vision-language pretraining, and anatomy-aware analysis of non-contrast chest CT.
